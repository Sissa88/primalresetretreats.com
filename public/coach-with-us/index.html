Help hold the mirror.

Primal Reset is developing a selective opportunity for women coaches and facilitators who can offer grounded, direct, human feedback.

This is not a decorative role. It calls for emotional maturity, clear boundaries, relevant experience, and respect for every participant's dignity.

The role

Presence over performance.

Observe

Notice posture, communication, and group dynamics without reducing a participant to a diagnosis.

Reflect

Offer candid, compassionate feedback that helps men understand how their presence is experienced.

Facilitate

Lead within a clearly defined scope, support group safety, and coordinate closely with the retreat team.
