The Men's Rebirth

Seven days on Hawaiʻi Island to rebuild confidence, presence, and direction—with guided practice and honest feedback to help you feel comfortable connecting with women again.

Dates announced soon Big Island, Hawaiʻi 15 men

Who this is for

A deliberate pause for the man who knows the old way is no longer working.

The life you had ended. Yours didn't.

Maybe you're moving through divorce, rejection, burnout, isolation, or the quiet realization that you no longer recognize yourself.

This retreat is for men ready to participate—not perform. You do not need to arrive with answers. You do need honesty, respect for the group, and a willingness to engage.

The connection lab

Most men never get honest feedback from women. Here, you will.

You begin a real conversation with a woman coach. She reflects what she actually experienced—your posture, voice, eye contact, pace, attention, and emotional presence. You adjust, then practice again.

The goal is not a pickup technique. It is learning to create genuine connection without pretending, chasing, or shrinking.

  • Practice a grounded first interaction
  • Receive candid, respectful feedback
  • Adjust and try again
  • Build presence you can take home

The seven-day journey

A progression, not a checklist.

Day 01The Arrival

Welcome, orientation, posture baseline, opening fire, breathwork, and the first honest conversation.

Day 02The Primal Body

Beach movement, breathwork, cold immersion, lava terrain, focus practices, fire cooking, rhythm, and expression.

Day 03The Mirror

Kīlauea’s volcanic landscape, reflection, and a coached connection lab with direct, respectful feedback.

Day 04The Dream

Sunrise practice, a future-vision ritual, island exploration, physical challenge, and a new personal rhythm.

Days 05–06The Warrior Camp

A men-only wilderness chapter centered on practical challenge, story, self-responsibility, rest, and a personal code.

Day 07The New Man

Final movement, closing ritual, commitments, documentation of progress, and the beginning of ongoing brotherhood.

All activities remain subject to final confirmation, participant screening, weather and site conditions, and safety review.

Your base

Open Gate. Cooled lava underfoot. Sky in every direction.

Open Gate is an off-grid basecamp on Hawaiʻi Island's southeastern lava fields. Accommodations are simple and communal rather than resort-style, with access to the coast, jungle, and volcanic landscapes.
