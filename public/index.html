The setting

Built on land that knows how to begin again.

Open Gate is an off-grid basecamp set directly on cooled lava fields on Hawaiʻi Island's southeastern coast. Accommodations are simple and communal rather than resort-style, surrounded by volcanic earth, jungle, and ocean.

Why it exists

“Too many good men disappear into themselves when life falls apart.”

Dan created Primal Reset after watching friends lose their confidence, connection, and momentum after divorce.

His vision is a human-scale experience that invites men to listen without judgment, face challenges without humiliation, and build friendships that continue after everyone flies home.

Questions, honestly answered

Before you step in.

Is this pickup training?+

No. There are no canned lines, tricks, or pressure tactics. The goal is to help you become more grounded, aware, and comfortable during genuine human interaction. You practice starting conversations and receive respectful feedback about how you come across.

Will feedback from women be embarrassing?+

The exercises may feel uncomfortable at first, but they are designed to be candid, respectful, and constructive. You are not being judged or publicly humiliated. You are getting useful information, practicing again, and building confidence through experience.

Is this therapy?+

No. The concept is a facilitated personal-development retreat built around movement, reflection, coaching, and peer connection. Final screening and support details will be confirmed before launch.

Do I need to be extremely fit?+

The finished retreat will publish clear physical requirements and adaptations. The intention is meaningful challenge, not competition or performance.

Can I reserve a place now?+

Not yet. This is a private concept preview. The production experience will begin with a short application and fit conversation before any deposit is accepted.

What is included?+

The current plan includes lodging, island transportation connected to the retreat, programmed experiences, and meals. Exact inclusions, pricing, and policies are still being finalized.
