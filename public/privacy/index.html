Privacy and email preferences

Updated November 17, 2026

Booking and inquiry details

We use your name and email to manage your booking or respond to your inquiry. Retreat inquiries also collect age, country, and an optional phone number. These contact details are stored privately in our Cloudflare D1 database. Paid bookings retain payment references and the amounts paid and owed.

Contact messages are processed by our form service and sent to our inbox. The customer database does not store your message text or health information. Please avoid including sensitive health information in the contact form.

Payments

PayPal processes your payments. Card numbers and security codes are entered in PayPal-hosted fields and are not stored in our customer database. A cardholder may pay for someone else; the guest’s contact details identify the person attending.

Optional marketing emails

Marketing is a separate choice. The checkbox is optional and starts unchecked. Only tick it if you are the person named and want occasional retreat news, offers, and updates. You can book or inquire without subscribing.

We record your choice, the wording you agreed to, its version, and when it was recorded. We do not sell your contact details. Any marketing mailing must include an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops marketing; it does not cancel a booking or prevent necessary booking and payment messages.

Your choices

Use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email, or email info@primalresetretreats.com to withdraw consent, correct your details, or request access or deletion. Some booking or payment records may need to be retained for business and legal obligations. We review these requests individually.
