Booking and cancellation terms

Men’s Rebirth Retreat · Primal Reset Retreats
Policy version: November 17, 2026

Your deposit and balance

A 50% deposit reserves one retreat place after payment is verified. Any valid promotion is applied to the full package price before the deposit is calculated. A private-room booking is for one guest in one room; a shared-room booking is for one guest in one bed.

The remaining balance is due February 1, 2027. It will be collected separately by invoice; your card is not automatically charged for the balance.

If you cancel

  • Within 7 days of booking: you receive a full refund if your booking was made at least 60 days before the retreat starts.
  • After that refund window, and at least 30 days before the retreat starts: the deposit is retained. Any retreat payment above the deposit is refunded.
  • Less than 30 days before the retreat starts: retreat payments are non-refundable.

Email your cancellation request to info@primalresetretreats.com. Cancellation timing is based on when we receive your request, using Hawaiʻi time.

If we postpone or cancel

The retreat requires at least 10 guests to proceed. If we postpone because fewer than 10 guests book, or if we cancel the retreat, you may choose a new retreat date or a full refund of your retreat payments. Moving your booking to another date is your choice.

Before booking flights

Please wait for our confirmation that the retreat will proceed before booking flights. Confirm your arrival, departure, and airport transfer arrangements with us before purchasing travel.

Refunds and questions

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. Contact info@primalresetretreats.com with your booking reference for cancellation, refund, or payment questions. These terms do not limit rights that apply under mandatory law.
